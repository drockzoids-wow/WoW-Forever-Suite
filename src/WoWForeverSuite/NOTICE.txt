Inventory tooltip presentation is adapted from the user's ZoidsTools addon.
The character roster's columns, class accents and hover details reference its Retail warband view.
Nearby quest-item selection uses ZoidsTools_F as a behavior reference, with Suite's own compact icon presentation.
Keybinding registration follows its standard WoW CLICK binding setup.
The minimap drawer uses its original-button collection behavior as a reference.
Action keybind abbreviations and the heavier size/shadow treatment also follow its behavior.
Following the Windows audio device uses Retail ZoidsTools as a behavior reference with an original Suite implementation.
Profession modifier actions, castbar controls and zone reputation use prior ZoidsTools behavior review as references; their Suite implementations are original.
Quest pickup uses Retail ZoidsTools quest-data readiness as a behavior reference with an original Suite implementation.
Its class and shared-bank icons use artwork provided by the WoW client.
